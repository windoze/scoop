Civil date conversion formulas by Howard Hinnant.
Source: https://howardhinnant.github.io/date_algorithms.html
Document date: 2021-09-01

The document's Summary donates these formulas to the public domain:
"Consider these donated to the public domain."

Scoop adapts the days_from_civil and civil_from_days formulas to its checked
year domain and explicit integer types. No C++ library or platform time code
is included.
